Course description

Course description

Fundamental concepts and techniques of computer programming using a high-level, object-oriented programming language. The course develops problem-solving skills, algorithm design and the use of systems software.

Topics include arrays, searching and sorting, classes and objects, encapsulation, inheritance and polymorphism, exception handling, file input and output, abstract classes and interfaces, and recursion.

Student learning outcomes

Learning outcomes

  1. Define and describe computing and programming concepts.
  2. Implement a computing-based solution to meet a given set of computing constructs.
  3. Demonstrate an understanding of problem-solving processes and design algorithms.
  4. Design, code, execute, test, debug and document programs in a high-level, object-oriented programming language.

Academic expectations

Course policies

Supplemental Instruction (SI)

The syllabus requires students in CPS 1231/2231 to attend at least 7 SI sessions during the semester, including 3 within the first 3 weeks. Book an appointment in TutorTrac before attending; attendance is recorded there.

SI appointment guidance (opens in a new tab)

Academic integrity & generative AI

AI-generated content must not be presented as original student work. Where the instructor permits AI use, acknowledge and cite it, and take responsibility for the accuracy and appropriateness of submitted work. Follow any additional assignment-specific rules.

University academic integrity policy (opens in a new tab)

Course materials & intellectual property

The syllabus limits class materials to the personal use of registered students during the semester. Do not publicly post or distribute homework, programs or other classwork. Follow the instructor’s permissions for each resource.

Communication & technology

Use a valid Kean email account when contacting your instructor, and check Canvas regularly. For account or technology issues, contact ithelpdesk@wku.edu.cn.

Accessibility & accommodations

Students who need instructional accommodations should notify the instructor as soon as possible and no later than the second week of the term. Contact oas@wku.edu.cn to discuss special needs and arrangements.
